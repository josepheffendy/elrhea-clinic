# Elrhea Clinic — product context

## Product purpose
Public website for Elrhea Clinic, a wellness clinic at Jl. Bendo No. 3, Lempongsari, Gajahmungkur, Semarang. The site exists to turn an Instagram or Google visitor into a WhatsApp booking (0819 7788 8850). There is no online booking system; WhatsApp is the conversion.

## Users
Semarang adults (roughly 30–55) who feel chronically tired, sleep badly or want to stay fit, mostly arriving on a phone from the Instagram bio or a Google Maps listing. They are cautious about medical claims and want to see that the place is real, clean and run by health professionals before they message.

Scene: a Semarang office worker, tired after a long week, taps the link in Elrhea's Instagram bio on her phone in the evening, half-lying on the sofa, deciding whether to WhatsApp for a Saturday slot.

## Services (as published by the clinic)
- Terapi Ozone (O3, Ozonette device) — the lead service on Instagram
- Vitamin Booster (infus vitamin), often combined with ozone
- Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy (HBOT)
- Aesthetic laser: Pico Laser, CO2 Laser

## Brand
- Logo: ER monogram in a roman serif with a gold four-point sparkle; wordmark ELRHEA / CLINIC in navy with gold rules.
- Instagram and signage: white monogram on royal blue. Royal blue and navy are the brand; gold is only the sparkle.
- Voice: Bahasa Indonesia, warm and plain ("kamu"), calm, never pushy. Mirrors the clinic's Instagram captions.

## Tone and constraints
- Health-advertising rules (Permenkes 1787/2010): no superlatives, no guaranteed outcomes, no patient testimonials, always recommend consultation with medical staff first.
- No invented facts: no stats, ratings, doctor names or prices until the clinic supplies them. Unknowns are visibly marked placeholders.

## Anti-references
- Indonesian beauty-clinic template: bright pill buttons, icon-grid treatment menus, stat badges, stock model faces (Venice, Seby, Miracle).
- The original Colorlib "Energen" template this site started from.

## References the owner likes
- zapclinic.com header: one clean row, large readable menu with dropdowns, solid booking button.
- prodia.co.id/id/klinik: feels like a "real website" with proper navigation, breadcrumbs, a real clinician photo.
